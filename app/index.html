C Clio Notes

Meeting

Captures your microphone as “Me”, your computer’s sound as “Others”, and the screen you chose in Settings. Speech is transcribed, each new screen is read, and notes are written section by section while the meeting runs. Make sure everyone knows the meeting is being recorded.

Settings

Choose what to record and which AI providers do the work.

Recording

System audio (“Others”) is always taken from your default speakers or headphones.

Jobs

Clio Notes runs on your own API keys. Each job uses one provider profile. Keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager, never in files.
